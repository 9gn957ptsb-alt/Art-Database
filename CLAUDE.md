Ponytail is on (the artist asked for it, 22 Oct 2026): on every coding task, follow
`.claude/skills/ponytail/SKILL.md` at its default level, full. The other ponytail skills
(review, audit, debt, gain, help) are in `.claude/skills/` too.

Words only (the artist, 23 Oct 2026): "all you need from me is words, i.e., there should not be
anything you need from me besides my words". Never ask the artist to type a command, open settings
or move files. Do it with the tools at hand; where only their own computer can do it, write the one
sentence they can say to the Claude running there. Decide what a sensible default decides, and ask
only for what only their words can give. Anything they must paste elsewhere goes in one copy-ready
code block, with where to paste it ("send it to me like that so I get exactly what you want and it's
easiest for me to do it", 23 Oct 2026).
