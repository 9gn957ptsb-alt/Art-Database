## Front page peels away on rotation

> I want the front page to peel away upon rotating the token object like your
> ducking into the shadows of a tree, patterned in blues, upon escaping the
> beating light of day

Kept in the artist's words rather than paraphrased — the image is the brief.

What it implies technically: rotation stops being a per-work control and becomes
the page's own gesture. The front page is a surface that gives way, and what is
behind it is cooler, dappled, blue-patterned — shade rather than a different page.
Light and shadow carry the transition, not a slide or a fade.

Unresolved, and only the artist can settle it: **what the token object is.** The
current rotate control is a small button per collage. This reads as something
singular and central to the front page — one object the visitor turns. Whether
that is one of the works, an emblem, or something else is the first question.

Related: the per-collage rotation now on the reference page is a different
mechanism. Whether these become one gesture or stay separate is open.
