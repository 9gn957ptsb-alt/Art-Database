Holographic Mr Pearl — the artist on the world's size and every player's world (verbatim, then the working)

15 Oct 2026: "At some point, the size of the world becomes arbitrary, understanding how fast we can go in holographic
mode, determines how much time it will take to explore or be able to explore an entire planet. Someone doesn't need to
see the entire planet, and using the generative quality of artificial intelligence everyone's world is going to be a
slightly different world, but the mission will be the same and that's what makes it a game."

Rules:
1. The hologram's speed (with the time limit) sets how long a planet takes to explore, and whether it can be.
2. No one needs to see the whole planet: past the size a player can cover, the planet's size is arbitrary.
3. Every player's world is generated, so each is slightly different (AI's generative quality), but the mission is the
   same for everyone: that is what makes it a game.

Working (for the environment, after the unit):
- One trip as the hologram reaches v x T (speed x time limit). The body can then be moved to where the head is, so the
  body leapfrogs a trip at a time; the cost of each trip is the body left vulnerable.
- Time to go round a planet of radius R is 2 pi R / v; to see all of it (a swath of the view's width) 4 pi R^2 / (2 w v).
- Today: walking 4.3 m/s (2.2 in water), the world 384 m x 384 m, the view 420 m. The song is 2 min 30 s.

  Seeing all of it (420 m swath): R 5 km at 80 m/s 1.3 h; R 10 km at 80 m/s 5.2 h. So from about R 5-10 km at
  hologram speeds no one sees it all in a sitting, and the size is arbitrary: the planet is generated where the player
  goes, from that player's own information (their information is the seed), not stored whole.
- "Dancing with jet planes" (the song): the hologram can keep up with jets at its fastest, about 250 m/s.
- Same mission for all (the reason he must leave, and the way off: the body to space in the rocket; the hologram
  leaving the planet does not count); each world differs in its land, its places, the giants and craters, where the
  pieces of the way off are.
