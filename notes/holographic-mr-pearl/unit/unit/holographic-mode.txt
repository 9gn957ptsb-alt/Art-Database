Holographic mode — the artist, 15 Oct 2026 (verbatim, then the rules as understood)

"I want a Holographic mode where you leave Mr Pearls body and can freeroam anywhere and this mode will be how you
introduce portals into the game. The catch is when you go into holographic mode Mr Pearl's body is completely
vulnerable to getting destroyed. We can also introduce a time limit to Holographic mode. When you go into holographic
mode, you can stop at any given point and choose to move Mr Pearl's body exactly there. There is no fall damage,
however, you cannot complete the missions like going to space by merely going into holographic mode and venturing
outside of the planet. perhaps the first planet is more so an exercise of everything you are capable of in the game."

Rules:
1. Holographic mode: Mr Pearl's hologram leaves his body and free-roams anywhere (no collision limits as a body has).
2. Portals come into the game through this mode.
3. While he is out, his body stays where it was and is completely vulnerable: it can be destroyed.
4. The mode has a time limit.
5. At any point while out, he can stop and move his body to exactly where the hologram is.
6. No fall damage (a body moved into the air, or falling, is not hurt by the fall).
7. Missions cannot be completed by holographic mode alone: leaving the planet as a hologram does not count (going
   to space needs the rocket, the body).
8. The first planet is the exercise of everything he can do in the game: a training world.
To settle later: what threatens the body (the giants? falling craters?), how long the limit is, what portals are and do,
what the hologram can and cannot touch.

Added 15 Oct 2026 (the artist): "Holographic mode disengages the head of Mr Pearl. His head/Holographic mode will be the
only perfect Sphere in the game, everything else will receive the consequential texture of the base unit we are
figuring out"
-> The hologram is his head: it lifts off and free-roams; the headless body stays behind, vulnerable. The head is the
   only perfect sphere in the game; everything else, his body included, is built from the base unit (design-rules.txt).
