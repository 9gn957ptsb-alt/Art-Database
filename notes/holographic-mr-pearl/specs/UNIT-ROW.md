# The unit's studies in a row beside the tree (design, 22 Oct 2026)

The artist, 21 Oct 2026: "Regarding the unit studies, I want those units [studies] to be placed in holographic Mr.
[Pearl] right next to the tree lined up next to each other".

What each study can show is mapped in the workflow's read (scratchpad `unit_map.json`; the studies' own notes in
`notes/holographic-mr-pearl/unit/`). The row takes the studies that make a thing you can walk round, each the study's
own result, each on the homage's kind of mount and saying, from its white stone, the relation it found:

| # | study | the thing | its size | from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | arnell/unit, u1 (ARNELL 1) | **the crux**: the figure at levels 2, 3 and 4 (scaled F(m+4)/8: 1, 13/8, 21/8) about the drawing's middle and moved up its drawing by R4 - Rm, so the three apexes meet at the crux from the stone; each with its holding circle, the rings the sight draws | about 3 m across at the homage's 0.55 m to a cell | homage.json `one` boxes, scaled and moved (no Python) |
| 2 | wf6/levels | **the ladder**: a level-4 frame, three level-3 frames at its corners, nine leaves, and the inverted copies in the openings (13 copies, 117 boxes), strict layering (A, B, C stepped back along the line of sight, so they stay boxes) | 3.3 m | levels.json's formulas, a few lines of JS at plan time |
| 3 | wf6/reconstruct | **V3, the closed curved form**: nine bars, each bent back a third of the way along the line of sight where nothing in the drawing covers it, so it closes for real (4e-16) | 0.86 m box, 1.1 m across its drawing | form.json from write_form.py (numpy), 882 hexes as a mesh |
| 4 | wf6/solids | **the sphere of the unit**: every block whose centre lies within F(4) = 3 of the middle, 136 in six layers 12 24 32 32 24 12; the pearl he throws, a metre to the cell | 6 m | the place's own blocks (w.set), as the homage sets its limestone |

Left out, and why: wf6/colour (its films need a shader that was lost; in form it is the homage's first figure again);
wf6/pearl (the headless body would read as the player's own body left behind; it is offered to the artist instead).

## Where

Beside the tree you wake under, "right next to the tree": a row square to the blocks, starting two metres from the
trunk on the side away from the wreck and the homage, the four 6 m apart (the homage's SPACING), each stone on your
side. Planned as the homage is (homage.js `layout`): level open ground, the air above clear, nothing between each
stone's eye and its figure; where there is no room beside the tree, the next ring out.

## What each says

From its white stone (as the homage's): its name, then the study's one or two sentences (unit_map.json `says`), with
the relation that sets its size.
