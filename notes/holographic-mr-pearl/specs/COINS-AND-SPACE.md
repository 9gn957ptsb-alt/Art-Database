# Coins, chasers and space (scoping, 22 Oct 2026; not built)

The artist's request of 21 Oct 2026 is quoted in full in the session brief (queue item 3). In parts, each to be built,
tested and published on its own, in this order:

1. **Half the space.** "cut the surrounding space down in half for now to make the development of it more
   efficient": each place's world halved across (buildings and new forms 256 → 128 m, cities 384 → 192, museums
   320–512 → 160–256), the model's own plot and the crash site kept whole. To check: the largest plots still fit;
   the homage's and the studies' rows still find room (check_homage.js); loading time.
2. **Ten coins and their tally.** "like sonic ... all through the space, inside and outside the architecture";
   collected "in either the holographic mode or 3rd person mode"; "keep a tally". Proposed: the coin is the figure
   in the guitar rib's gold, half a block across, turning a turn a beat as Sonic's rings turn; ten in a world, five
   inside the building (its rooms, the largest first) and five outside (roofs, lots, the tree), laid by the
   adventure's seed so the same world always has the same ten; touched by his body's box or by the hologram's head;
   "Coins 3 of 10" in the header (live text), kept in the save.
3. **The chasers.** "Think of Mr Pearl like the golden snitch ... there are people trying to grab me". Proposed: the
   guards made visible, three (the three alarms), built of the unit, walking at his walk over φ (2.66 m/s) so he
   outruns them and the hologram outflies them; a grab of his body puts him out under his tree, as the guards do
   now, his coins kept. Open: whether a grab costs coins; whether they come only once an alarm has gone.
4. **The coins' way out.** "collecting all 10 coins equivalent to achieving building the rocket ship and blasting
   off". At ten, the rocket stands at the wreck as when the four pieces are found; he rides it up (where now he
   watches from his tree), steered by the stick; past "a certain threshold in the sky" (proposed: the height of the
   figure in the sky, the one point it closes from being the tree) control goes and the camera watches him blast off.
5. **Space.** "in his rockets ... shoot 10 enemy spacecraft down as they fly by ... once you shoot 10 you get shot by
   an enemy aircraft and crash down back to the planet where you have to assemble the rocket either by collecting 10
   coins or finding all the parts". Third person behind him in the pilot seat, the windshield's frame, the tips of
   the ship's guns and their lasers; "a reflex target symbol ... slightly below the center of the windshield" (the
   sight's figure mark, reused); the stick flies the ship, a button fires. Its own mode in the same canvas: a field of
   the NASA deep-space photographs' galaxies (the site's remains, docs/v2/land.js), enemies built of the unit.
   After the tenth, the hit and the fall: he comes to under his tree, coins and pieces to find again.

Each part answers to "THIS IS ABOUT DETERMINED RELATIONS": every count, speed and size above is to be set by a stated
relation when it is built, and said where it is tapped.
