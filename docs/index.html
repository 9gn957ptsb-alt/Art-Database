Statement

I am curious about what faculties are and how they pertain to the world. I explore the immediacy of visual perception through the colors and movements of abstraction. I practice this abstract perspective through collage, which I first developed through my process notebooks and modeling materials in architectural school. My collage practice has since evolved into one focusing on ordinary objects and how they come to reinforce perceptual forms relative to day to day activities. They can be seen as compressions of the spatial processions forming my identity. Furthermore, they are able to be as radically different from each other as the situations they are derived from—they do different things as they are about different sequences of events. My long term goal is to clarify why the very disciplinary boundaries and social shapes we practice reflect the very perceptual forms we look at them with.
