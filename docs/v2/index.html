The Artist Website of Matthew Livingston is one page with one address. The globe is the Earth. The pills at its foot choose what it carries: Collages, the artist's own works at their places; Museums, the cities that hold the museums with the works he saved; Architecture, the buildings he keeps; or Artists, each saved artist at their birthplace. Search, beside them, finds any work, artist, place, museum, show or writer. With the globe focused, the arrow keys turn it, plus and minus bring it nearer and farther, and Enter opens the place nearest the middle; in a city the same keys move its map. Tab moves between the places named. Pressing a place goes down to it: a city with its museums, its galleries and a guide to its shows; a museum with its saved works and a walk through the building; a work with everywhere it has been; an artist's life; a movement. Each view begins with its heading, then three ways on from it. The round dial is the view's years: its arrow keys turn it, and Space pauses or plays a path being played. Escape, minus or the way back at the top go one level up. Hold still, the first stop in the tab order or the word "still" in Search, stops everything that moves by itself.
