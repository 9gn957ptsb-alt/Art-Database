Turning the world…

Hold to condense

Matthew Livingston

A world made of the words the collages are made of. Every word is standing on the works that share it. Turn it, and something is grazing up there — it will turn up colours to throw.

The works

Drag to turn the world

Press and hold what it turns up to condense it into its three colours, then drag those colours onto the creature to repaint it. With a keyboard, press Enter on it to do both at once. Tab through the words to turn the world to each in turn.
