Turning the world…

Hold to condense

Open on Artsy

Press and hold what it turns up to condense it into its three colours, then drag those colours onto the creature to repaint it. With a keyboard, press Enter on it to do both at once. Each throw repaints three of the creature's twenty parts, so it carries several works at once; tab or hover onto a painted part to hear which work gave it that colour, and open it. Tab through the words to turn the world to each in turn. Drag anywhere to turn the world by hand.
