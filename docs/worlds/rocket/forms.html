The buildings the site has modelled, from the Architectural Authority

Forms

Each of the thirty-six is taken apart into the sections it is made of: its volumes, each named by the space it is (the ground floor, a wing, the long wing, the glass pavilion, the hall, the tower, the block, the upper box, the cantilevered box, the lookout, the room of an interior); its roofs; the features that make it itself rather than a box (screens, slats, fins, colonnades, pergolas, canopies, walls); its pools, terraces and garden. Then they are put together again, in ways their architects never did. Every building in Rocket's world is one of these new forms.

New forms

One project's volume on the ground, another's wings against it, a third's upper box on top run out past its edge as far as it ran out in its own building, a fourth's roof drawn again over it at its own pitch, a fifth's screen across a face, a pool slid under the overhang, and the trees of a garden round it. Each is made by a number; each can be escaped from.

Taken apart

Each project drawn taken apart (tap it to put it together), and each of its sections in its blocks, a metre to the block, every block three faces in three tones of its colour, as the tribar has them. A bay that repeats along a building is one section, with how many times it comes.

Made by scripts/build_sections.js, which reads the sections from the models' own parts, and forms.js, which puts them together; both run in the page and in Node. Back to Rocket.
