The artist's figure: three tribars round a gap

The figure in 3D

The drawing shows the figure from one direction only, and says nothing of how deep anything in it is. Every point of an object can slide along that line of sight, or be moved with all the others on its sightline, and the drawing does not change: one drawing is a whole family of objects, most of which never touch themselves where the drawing says they do. These are ten of them, most made of the figure with itself. Turn each by hand; then let it swing round to the one place it closes.
