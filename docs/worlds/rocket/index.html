Where they fell

      A city of the Artist Website, in blocks

      Rocket

      You come down hard in a city and wake under a tree beside your capsule. Four pieces of your rocket are somewhere in the streets, on the roofs, under the parks and in the water. Find them, build the rocket at the wreck, and launch.

      Loading the cities

      On a phone: the stick on the left, drag to look, tap a block. On a computer: W A S D, drag to look, click; space jumps, 1–5 picks a block, E switches break and place.
