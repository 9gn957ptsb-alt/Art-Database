    Rocket

    You come down hard and wake under a tree beside your capsule. The four pieces of your rocket are somewhere in the place: behind four of the works hung in a museum, on a building's roof and in its garden, or across a city. Read the notes, work out which work hides which piece, build the rocket at the wreck, and launch. Every building round you, and every new form, is made of the parts of the buildings the site has modelled.

    Loading the places

    Tap a work to read its label and look behind it; three wrong looks and the guards put you out. On a phone: the stick on the left, drag to look, tap. On a computer: W A S D, drag to look, click; space jumps, 1–5 picks a block, E switches break and place, N opens the notes.
