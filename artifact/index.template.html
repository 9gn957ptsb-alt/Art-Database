A search engine for every artwork saved to one Artsy account. Thumbnails can't load here, so each work is drawn from the three dominant colors Artsy extracted from it, at the true proportions of the object itself.

The collection by hue

Every field is indexed: title, artist and co-artists, nationality, artist dates, medium, category, gallery and year. Accents are folded, so cezanne finds Cézanne and durer finds Dürer; misspellings fall back to the nearest indexed word. Scope a term with artist:, title:, medium:, category:, gallery:, nationality: or year:, and quote an "exact phrase". Titles follow catalogue convention: italic, with n.d. where the work carries no date.
