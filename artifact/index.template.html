Color Middling

Every artwork saved to one Artsy account, read as color. Thumbnails can't load here, so each work is drawn from the three dominant colors Artsy extracted from it, at the true proportions of the object itself.

The collection by hue

4,969 works fetched from the Artsy API, normalized into SQLite, and rendered from dominant_colors plus width_cm/height_cm. Titles follow catalogue convention: italic, with n.d. where the work carries no date. Each entry links out to its page on Artsy.
