Theme one

Ancient, In Your Colours

Three objects built out of the collection itself. The theme was picked by the colours rather than chosen first: this collection is a third terracotta, ochre and umber, with deep reserves of bone and limestone, so ancient is what it can actually render. Press and hold any block to see the work that colour came from.

Nothing here is tinted. Every block is an exact hex from a saved artwork, and each one slowly walks its own cycle of near-identical colours — so the surface breathes like firelight while the object holds still, and a flat-looking area is really dozens of different works.

Why ancient and not Christmas: the palette was counted before anything was drawn. Candidate colours in the collection within a close tolerance of each target —
