Colour Middling Falls

Every colour in the collection, falling. The sheet opens on whites, drops through the blues, and travels the whole hue wheel before arriving back at the crest — one closed loop, no colour altered. Point at the water to hold it still; click any block to open the work that colour came from.

Under the cursor

Built from artwork_colors: every block is an unaltered hex from one saved work, so what you click is what the database holds. The fall itself comes from ordering and motion, never from tinting — colours are laid into a ribbon of cells ordered white → blue → around the hue wheel → back to white, and the window scrolls down it and wraps. Each column slips against its neighbours within a bounded range, which is what separates falling water from confetti. Streaks are real colours swapped in: pale ones where the water runs dark, dark ones where it runs pale, since white foam is invisible against ochre.
