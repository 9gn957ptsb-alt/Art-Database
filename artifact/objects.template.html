What you've recorded so far

Objects are generated by scripts/build_sprites.py, scripts/build_gerstner.py, scripts/build_falls.py and scripts/build_leaves.py, then merged into this one page by scripts/build_objects.py. Where each sits on the axis above, and what machinery it therefore shares with its kin, is declared once in scripts/motion.py. Every slot in a designed ramp is backed by real colours from the collection, and a whole region walks that cycle together, so a surface breathes rather than sparkles. Feedback is stored in the artifact's own database, not in your browser, which is what makes it readable later.
